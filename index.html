WHAT CPB IS

A shared project, not a storefront.

CPB is a community-run modded Minecraft project. Members build, explore, organize in-world ventures, and help decide what comes next. Its roots are in CTBE, the name used for the earlier community and seasons; the current project identity is Create: Project Blueprint.

WHERE THINGS STAND Season 1 is in development

The former CTBE S3.1 chapter launched on June 20, 2026, then hardware failures took it offline. CPB is preparing its next season; the seed, server setup, and in-game notice board are still being worked through. No launch date is posted.
