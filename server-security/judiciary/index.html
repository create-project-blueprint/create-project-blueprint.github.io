Independent Adjudication

Judges operate autonomously from executive staff and law enforcement (FBOC/FBIS). Every judgment is rendered strictly based on evidence and server law.

Presumption of Good Faith

Accidental contraption malfunctions (e.g. steam boiler explosions or runaway minecarts) are evaluated fairly to distinguish negligence from intentional griefing.

Rather than arbitrary punitive bans, the court prioritizes equitable restitution in brass ingots, material replacement, or supervised labor repair.

CTBE Judicial Code & Precedents

Chapter 1: The Principle of Restitution

Property damage caused by kinetic experiments must first be compensated in kind or in brass value before any punitive ban is considered.

Chapter 2: Blueprint Copyright Standard

Schematics created and certified by registered server companies belong to the original engineering firm and cannot be pirated for public schematicannons.

Chapter 3: Rail Immunity Act

Inter-city passenger and freight trains are classified as public infrastructure. Intentionally obstructing tracks with stationary contraptions is a federal felony.
