SEASON 3.1 WORLD NOTES

The Mainland

The Mainland page began as an access countdown for the planned July 1, 2026 opening phase of the CTBE S3.1 world. The date has passed, but this page is now an archive note: the project history records that hardware failures later took the season offline.

It does not indicate that the Mainland server is currently open or that players can connect. CPB Season 1 is in development, with no public launch date posted.
